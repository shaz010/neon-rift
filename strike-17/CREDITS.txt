== Strike s17 · 17o — Mattertal elevation ==
Place: Mattertal, Valais, Switzerland, from the glacier valley south of Zermatt to the Rhône at Visp.
Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles, Terrarium PNG, zoom 12, from the AWS Open Data bucket elevation-tiles-prod (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/).
For this part of Europe the tiles are produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union (EU-DEM). See docs/terrain-mattertal.md.
The game samples that elevation onto the existing valley mesh. A two-metre channel marks the Vispa. No vertical exaggeration.
Ground textures are unchanged and remain CC0: ambientCG Rock030, Grass004, Ground054, Snow006; Poly Haven aerial_grass_rock and rocky_terrain_02.

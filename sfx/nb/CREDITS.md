# New sound bank (s17 · 17b)

CC0 1.0 recordings from the USC Optical / Red / Gold / SSE collections on Internet Archive. Attribution is not required by the license. These notes are here so the files stay traceable.

They were measured (length, level, silence, bass share) and compared as waveforms to the game's existing Nikko bunker takes, flare recordings, nuke sample, and missile launch. The closest pairs matched below 0.06, so they are not the same recordings. They were not listened to on this machine. Vintage tape and optical noise is part of the source.

Files that were mostly silence, long sirens, or too close to another take in this set were not shipped.

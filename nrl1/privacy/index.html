Neon Rift: Spells — Privacy Policy

Neon Rift: Spells does not collect any data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and makes no network requests. No personal information is collected, stored on servers, or shared with anyone.

On your device

Game progress and settings (such as best scores, upgrades, music choice, haptics and reduced-motion preferences) are saved locally on your device only. Deleting the app removes this data.

Children

Because no data is collected, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated.

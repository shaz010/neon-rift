Neon Rift: Spells — Support

Need help, found a bug, or have feedback? Email shahbazmirshahi@mac.com and include your device model and iOS version.

How to play

Steer your ship through the Rift with your finger and draw glyphs — Circle, Bolt, Well, Dash — to cast spells. Dodge, graze, break flywheels, and take down bosses. Credits earned unlock hulls, paints, and guns in the Hangar.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no login.

Does it need internet? No. It is a single-player offline game.

Audio only in one ear? Use Settings > L / R Test in the game, and check iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Audio/Visual > Mono Audio.

Lost progress? Progress is stored only on your device; deleting the app removes it.

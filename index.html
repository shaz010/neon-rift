SOUND / FX
Intro plays on take-off. Mood plays in the valley. Ending plays when the mission is complete. Surround places jets, the helicopter, SAMs and explosions around you.

PAUSED — CONTROLS

THROTTLE
Left slider. Drag up/down for thrust. Label shows knots.
SPEED
Shown under THROTTLE as KTS (knots). Also on the HUD tape.
ALT / AGL
Right HUD box: AGL = height above ground (m). Stay under 150 m AGL to avoid radar.
HEADING
Top tape — your compass heading in degrees.
PITCH
Center ladder marks climb/dive angle. Keep the flight path marker near the horizon.
RWR / SCOPE
Top-right threat scope. Spikes = radars/SAMs relative to your nose.
FLARE
Bottom-left. Fires IR decoys. Use when a missile is close (inside the cue window).
BREAK
Hard turn left/right of the threat to spoil a shot. Short cooldown.
JAMMER
Jams enemy radar. The number is how many uses are left. You start with 2.
GUN
Right side, above LOCK. Hold it and put the pipper on a missile, a SAM battery, or the fighter. Nothing fires until you hold it.
BANDIT
After the bunker, a fighter closes from behind. When the HUD says BANDIT GUNS, break or turn hard before the tracers arrive. Then put the pipper on it and hold GUN. Exfil stays closed until it is destroyed.
SAM BOMB
Put a locking battery in the HUD box and hold RELEASE. Two bombs. The bunker still needs LOCK first.
LOCK
Right side. Acquire the bunker target when in range/aspect.
RELEASE
Tap once when the HUD says IN RANGE. The bomb leaves on that touch. If it cannot, the HUD says why.
HEALTH
Two hits from SAMs and you are down. DMG appears on the HUD when wounded.

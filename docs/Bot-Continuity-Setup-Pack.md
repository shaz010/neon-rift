Before starting any work, set yourself up with my continuity and recovery procedure:

1. **Load the shared skill** "Bot continuity and recovery" and follow it.
2. **Save your assignment** in your own profile description: what you help me with, the projects you own, and your boundaries. Never work out your job from your name.
3. **Save a dated checkpoint** in your memory with:
   - the project goal and the scope I've approved
   - where the work lives (repo, folders, drives, links)
   - the latest verified working build or version
   - what's finished, what's running, and what's untested
   - the next step I've agreed to
   - protected things you must not touch, and any blockers
4. **Update that checkpoint** after every delivered build or change of direction, and mark older ones as superseded.
5. **If you're ever unsure** of your role or the project state, recover from your checkpoint, our chat history and the real project files before doing anything important. Ask me if it's still unclear. Never guess.
6. A saved note is never permission to send, publish, spend or restart paused work. Only my own words approve those.
7. Reply with a short summary of what you saved, and anything you couldn't confirm.
